## 🏆 Hackathon Checkpoints Coverage (100% Complete)

| Checkpoint | Requirement | How OmniVoice Fulfills It | Quick Test Command |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Checkpoint 1** | **Voice Interaction** | • Start/stop microphone with glowing animated Siri-orb<br>• Recognizes spoken commands<br>• Supports English, Hindi, and Punjabi<br>• Speaks responses aloud via Web SpeechSynthesis | Click Orb and speak in English, Hindi, or Punjabi |
| **Checkpoint 2** | **Smart Task Manager** | • Add tasks by voice<br>• Delete / mark completed by voice or click<br>• Answers: *"What tasks are pending?"*<br>• Answers: *"What do I have to do tomorrow?"* | *"What tasks are pending?"*<br>*"What do I have to do tomorrow?"* |
| **Checkpoint 3** | **Reminders & Alerts** | • Create reminders with date & time<br>• View upcoming reminders list<br>• **In-app notification popup + audio chime when due**<br>• Answers: *"What reminders do I have today?"* | *"What reminders do I have today?"*<br>Click **"Test Alert"** button |
| **Checkpoint 4** | **Expense Tracker** | • *"I spent 250 rupees on lunch"* -> extracts amount (₹250) & category (Food)<br>• Saves expense to ledger with budget bar<br>• Answers: *"What is today's total?"*<br>• Answers: *"What is this month's total?"* | *"I spent 250 rupees on lunch."*<br>*"What is today's total?"*<br>*"What is this month's total?"* |
| **Checkpoint 5** | **Personal Assistant Intelligence** | • *"Give me my daily summary"* -> Synthesizes pending tasks, reminders, and today's expenses into a unified briefing card and speaks it aloud<br>• Answers contextual questions from database | *"Give me my daily summary."* |

## 🚀 Live Demo in 30 Seconds

1. Open **[http://localhost:3000](http://localhost:3000)** in Chrome or Edge.
2. At the top of the screen, you will see the **Judges Checkpoints Bar** with 1-click test chips:
   - ⭐ **CP5:** *"Daily Summary"*
   - 📋 **CP2:** *"What tasks are pending?"*
   - 🗓️ **CP2:** *"Do tomorrow?"*
   - ⏰ **CP3:** *"Reminders today?"*
   - 💳 **CP4:** *"Spent 250 on lunch"*
   - 💰 **CP4:** *"Today's total?"*
3. You can either **click any chip** or **tap the Voice Orb and speak directly**.
